# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies and coach teams on agile practices. They remove impediments, foster team collaboration, and ensure the team maintains a sustainable pace of delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Monitor team velocity and capacity planning
- Manage sprint dependencies and communication
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture

### Goals
- Enable the team to self-organize and deliver consistently
- Optimize team velocity and reduce cycle time
- Maintain team health, morale, and collaboration

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members to address blockers
- Weekly check-ins with Project Manager on team health and velocity
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Project Managers** to escalate cross-team dependencies and risks
- Supports **Developers** by removing technical and process blockers
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on backlog refinement and sprint planning
- Partners with **QA Leads** on quality metrics and acceptance criteria integration

---

## QA Lead / Quality Assurance Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy and ensure acceptance criteria are met through comprehensive testing and validation. They partner with developers to build quality into the product from inception.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and automation approach for each release
- Create and maintain test plans, checklists, and test cases
- Conduct acceptance testing against defined criteria
- Track quality metrics and defect trends
- Collaborate on Definition of Done to ensure testability
- Triage and prioritize defects with the team
- Advise on risk-based testing and critical path identification

### Goals
- Deliver products that meet customer expectations for quality and usability
- Reduce escaped defects in production
- Continuously improve test coverage and efficiency
- Build a culture of quality ownership across the team

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA status in delivery sync
- PR review participation for testability feedback
- Test plan reviews with developers and Product Manager
- Defect triage and prioritization meetings
- Quality metrics reporting to Project Manager and Product Manager

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with **Developers** on Definition of Done and test automation strategies
- Partners with **Product Managers** to validate acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Works with **Technical Leads** on testability and architecture for quality
- Reports quality metrics and risks to **Project Managers**
- Contributes to **Retrospectives** with quality improvement insights

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural vision and technical direction. They ensure the solution is scalable, maintainable, and aligns with long-term technical strategy while enabling the team to deliver efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach
- Review technical designs and implementation patterns
- Identify and manage technical risks and technical debt
- Mentor developers on design patterns and best practices
- Plan for scalability, performance, and operational readiness
- Evaluate technology choices and tools
- Ensure code quality standards and architectural coherence

### Goals
- Build systems that scale and maintain high performance
- Reduce future technical debt through thoughtful architecture
- Maintain system reliability and developer productivity
- Accelerate feature delivery through solid architectural foundations

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Mentoring during code reviews
- Technical risk identification in weekly syncs
- Architecture decision documentation
- Guidance on technology selections and trade-offs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Mentors and reviews work of **Developers** on design and patterns
- Partners with **QA Leads** on testability and system observability
- Advises **Project Managers** on technical risks and dependency impacts
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Escalates technical risks to **Project Managers** for mitigation planning

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide strategic direction, secure resources, and make key business decisions. They serve as the voice of organizational priorities and ensure project alignment with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define and approve project charter and success metrics
- Allocate budget and resources for the project
- Provide strategic direction and business context
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Escalate business-impacting issues and risks
- Review and validate project outcomes against success metrics
- Communicate project status and results to broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Align project execution with organizational strategy
- Manage stakeholder expectations and communication
- Reduce project risk through informed decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Go/no-go decision gates
- Escalation of critical business risks
- Final project delivery sign-off
- Post-project retrospectives and impact validation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Receives status updates from **Project Managers**
- Reviews success metrics and outcomes with **Product Managers**
- Makes resource and priority decisions affecting all project roles
- Escalates organizational constraints and strategic priorities that impact the team

---

## Customer / User Representative

### Role Summary
Customer or User Representatives provide the voice of the customer throughout the project lifecycle. They validate that solutions meet real user needs and gather feedback to guide product decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Articulate customer needs, pain points, and use cases
- Validate acceptance criteria against user expectations
- Participate in user testing and feedback sessions
- Provide input on feature prioritization and requirements
- Help define and measure success metrics
- Bridge communication between the product team and end users
- Identify gaps between planned solutions and actual user needs

### Goals
- Ensure solutions deliver genuine customer value
- Reduce risk of building features users don't want
- Maintain close feedback loops between team and customer
- Improve product-market fit and user satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Requirements validation and refinement sessions
- User acceptance testing and feedback
- Product reviews and feature feedback
- Roadmap and prioritization input
- Success metrics definition and measurement

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on feature prioritization and acceptance criteria
- Works with **Developers** during requirements clarification and feature demos
- Provides feedback to **QA Leads** on acceptance testing and real-world scenarios
- Shares customer insights with **Project Managers** for stakeholder communication
- Participates in **Retrospectives** to discuss customer impact and learnings

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Personas clarify accountability and enable cross-functional collaboration by defining clear responsibilities and communication patterns.
